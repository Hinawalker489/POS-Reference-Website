FAQs

Answers to the most common POS questions.

Does the POS work offline?

Yes. Transactions queue locally and sync once connectivity resumes, ensuring zero lost sales.

Can we use our existing hardware?

The platform supports standard Windows, Android, and iPad terminals along with USB/Bluetooth peripherals.

How secure is the system?

Every action is logged, role permissions are granular, and data is encrypted both in transit and at rest.
